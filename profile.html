Core Features

A look at the tools for accelerated growth and community.

🤖

AI Learning Buddy

This dedicated AI assistant serves as a personal reflection guide to facilitate self-directed learning.

  • »
    Weekly Reflection Prompts: Delivers prompts designed to encourage introspection (e.g., "What was your proudest moment this week?").
  • »
    Smart Suggestions: Uses growth data to **recommend personalized resources** like articles, videos, and skill challenges.
👍

Peer Feedback Wall

A dynamic, positive-only digital space where classmates celebrate one another's soft skills.

  • »
    Community Appreciation: Students can **appreciate others** for demonstrated teamwork, kindness, or creative ideas.
  • »
    Measurable Development: Every "appreciation" automatically contributes to the recipient's **Character section** in their Growth Profile.
